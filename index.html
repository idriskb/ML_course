How these notes work

Each chapter is written like a mathematics text, with numbered definitions and theorems and complete proofs. Interactive figures sit next to the formal statements so you can test an idea before you prove it.

  • Rigorous foundations

    Numbered definitions, lemmas and theorems with cross-references, as in a textbook.

  • Proofs on demand

    Proofs are collapsed until you open them, so you can try each statement yourself first.

  • Step-by-step derivations

    Long computations are revealed one line at a time, each with a short justification.

  • Interactive figures

    Move sliders, step through algorithms and watch the mathematics respond.
